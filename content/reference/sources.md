This guide stands on a lot of other people's documentation. The most important citations are already woven through the site: every mod named in these pages links to its Nexus page, and that page (the author's own description and docs) is the primary source for what the mod does. When a write-up here and a mod's Nexus page disagree, trust the Nexus page&mdash;it may have been updated since this guide was written.

What follows is the bigger reference material: the documentation worth reading in its own right, collected in one place.
