These are the controller bindings from {{< nexus 49650 >}}Alternative VRIK Controller Bindings{{< /nexus >}}, offered in [Onboarding](/start/onboarding) as the SteamVR option {{< btn-inline >}}VRIK Controller Bindings - Easy Shout{{< /btn-inline >}}.

As the name suggests, this is the [VRIK scheme](/controls/bindings-vrik) with shouting handed its own dedicated button. Only two things differ from the standard VRIK bindings:

* **Shout** moves to the right {{< control name="b" >}} button (instead of {{< control name="a" >}}).
* **Activate** (interact) takes over the right {{< control name="a" >}} button (instead of the right grip).

Everything else (movement, combat, menus) matches the VRIK bindings, so if you know one, you know the other.
