With over 1,700 mods in the list, there are an awful lot of keybindings already assigned. This is a reference to the default inputs for mod actions throughout MGO, sorted by the kind of controller they're on. It's built from a spreadsheet compiled by community member **@DonQuibrote**. (Thanks!)

Type in the filter to search by input, mod, or action. Looking for everything a mod does? Filter by its name! Wondering what's making {{< control name="grip" >}} do something you didn't expect? Filter by the button!

{{< aside type="btw" title="Defaults, not ultimate truth" >}}
These are the default inputs set by each included mod. Many can be changed in the mod's MCM. (If you've already changed something, your game obviously won't match this list.)

A key or button that isn't listed here isn't necessarily free. Skyrim and OpenComposite Unleashed have their own bindings that aren't included here, and some mods' defaults couldn't be confirmed. For Skyrim's controls, see the [controller binding](/controls/) of your choice.
