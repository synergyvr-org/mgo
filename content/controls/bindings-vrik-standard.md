These are the controller bindings from the {{< nexus 23416 >}}VRIK Player Avatar{{< /nexus >}} bindings download, offered in [Onboarding](/start/onboarding) as the SteamVR option {{< btn-inline >}}VRIK Controller Bindings - Standard{{< /btn-inline >}}. They're the same scheme as the [VRIK bindings](/controls/bindings-vrik).

If you're weighing this against [VRIK Easy Shout](/controls/bindings-vrik-easy-shout), the difference is in three right-hand buttons:

* **Shout** is the right {{< control name="a" >}} button (Easy Shout moves it to {{< control name="b" >}}).
* **Activate** (interact) is the right {{< control name="right-grip" >}} (Easy Shout moves it to {{< control name="a" >}}).
* The right {{< control name="b" >}} button opens your **Favorites** (Easy Shout folds favorites into Activate to free up {{< control name="b" >}} for shouting).
