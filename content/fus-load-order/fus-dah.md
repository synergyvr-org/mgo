**FUS DAH** includes the VR basics from FUS along with some gameplay changes, but it does _not_ include the visual changes from FUS RO.

## What FUS DAH adds

This is where the gameplay overhauls arrive. Most of the additions are from the Simonrim suite (Adamant for perks, Mysticism for magic, Apothecary, Pilgrim, Mundus, Aetherius, etc.) alongside Blade and Blunt for combat, various NPC dialogue expansions, and a bunch of animations.

The handful of entries filed under _Late Load Visuals_ aren't really about beautification, for the most part. They're basically cleanup, removing or toning down effects and visual clutter like interior fog, cave dust[^1], and blowing snow.

[^1]: via the hilariously named "No More of that dumb falling dust thingy in caves"
