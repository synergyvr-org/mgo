**FUS RO DAH** includes the VR basics, visual improvements, and gameplay changes (VR-specific, and in general). It includes optional categories, but most are enabled by default. Deselect whichever optional mods you prefer.

## What FUS RO DAH adds

FUS RO DAH is a superset of FUS RO _and_ FUS RO DAH, so what it adds depends on where you're coming from.

### Coming from FUS RO

You already have the visuals, so what's new is the gameplay layer: the Simonrim overhauls, Blade and Blunt, the dialogue expansions, and the animations.

### More than the sum of its parts

You might expect FUS RO DAH to be exactly FUS RO and FUS DAH bolted together, and it _almost_ is. Nothing from either of the other lists gets dropped, but five mods appear here that are in neither of the others, and that happens to make for a nice illustration of why combining modlists is harder than just smashing two folders together.

Two of the additions are compatibility patches that only become necessary once both the visual enchancements and the gameplay overhauls are present: Lux (lighting, from RO) needs a patch to play nicely with Pilgrim (religion, from DAH), and another for Inigo. Speaking of our favorite feline follower, **Inigo** himself is added here, alongside his official patch.
